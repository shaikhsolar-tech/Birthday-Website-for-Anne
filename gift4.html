A message from me to you

Well, hey there Anne! It's your birthday today by the time you're reading this, but it's... been so nice ever since July 28. You know, the first time I approached you and you were sort of silent I didn't think you'd actually respond... And then I turn around one last time and I was surprised. Honestly, after that day I've been wondering how it would feel... to tell the people that we know our meeting depended solely upon the insurance that the internet would behave on that very day, along with everything that would come later. I used to... hate people, as I told you. Like not enough to kill them, I hope... gulp..., but it was enough for me to avoid talking. I'm all grateful and thankful to the fates for giving me the courage to talk that day.

I wish I could do more for you, and I would if I could, but distance limits me. I just want to tell you that I think I love you more than anything else I have loved in my life now... I was wondering whether you felt the same. I wish I could be there when you start cutting whatever you're getting so that I could be the first one to make sure most of it gets on your face rather than going into your stomach.

I've been thinking about your mother a lot, and well... Have been trying to change how I am in hopes that I could soothe her even out of the grave by loving her kid to the greatest extent I can no matter what and making sure her kid never felt an ounce of regret, hate, or neglect through her life. I am slowly working on that... And I hope I'll be getting even better with time. It's hopefully dumbfounding... I had no idea what you were when I met you, in fact I still don't really care about your background... But have to care anyway for the sake of how important it is to everyone and you.

The biggest sign of love you could give me is choosing a suburban boy from India who hopelessly dreams into your life and taking him as yours. I am still baffled by it, and I love you. There are like more than a trillion cells in my body right now, disregarding decay rates and such which mighttttt make the count differ, and I could say with the absolute truth that I'd let every single one of them be yours if you ever asked.

I want to be the man your mother would've hoped you found, even if I don't have much to provide physically... I want to ensure that the remaining part of your life is spent without any form of extreme tiredness, guilt, regret or even hate. I apologize if this paragraph is a bit too long, I know you once told me to sort of cut down on the paragraphs, but I had no idea how to compress it.

I love you Anne Antoine Lorent, and the first moment I see you I'm going to make sure you're the happiest person on earth and the happiest wife too (eventually 😉😝 I HOPE YOU DONT MIND the little emojis... I know you told me to not use them... But I couldn't resist teasing you a little)
